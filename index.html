Frequently Asked Questions

Is BG PDF Tools really free?

Yes. All tools are completely free with no hidden charges, watermarks or limits.

Do you upload my PDF files?

No. All processing happens directly in your browser using JavaScript. Your files never leave your device.

Can I use it offline?

After the page loads once, most tools continue to work even without internet.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Because nothing is uploaded to any server, your documents remain 100% private.
